# 🌐hotel-reservation-financial-report

This project delivers a comprehensive analytical dashboard for hotel reservation performance and financial insights. It combines operational booking metrics with revenue analysis to help hotel management identify trends, reduce revenue leakage, and improve decision making.

The report includes interactive visuals, advanced DAX calculations, custom flip‑flop KPI cards and tooltip‑based micro insights for deeper exploration of guest behavior and revenue performance.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Overview
The solution consists of two main analytical sections:

## 1) Reservation Performance Dashboard
Focuses on booking behavior, cancellations, seasonality, and monthly trends.

Key insights include:

* Total bookings vs last month & last year
* Successful vs cancelled bookings across seasons
* Year‑over‑year reservation vs cancellation comparison
* Monthly reservation and cancellation trends
* Top months with highest reservations and cancellations
* Weekly booking patterns across all months

These visuals help identify peak seasons, cancellation hotspots, and operational bottlenecks.

## 2) Financial Performance Dashboard
Provides a detailed breakdown of revenue earned vs revenue lost.

Includes:

* Gross Revenue, Net Revenue, Lost Revenue (MoM comparison)
* % Lost Revenue with automated alert indicator
* Revenue distribution across nationalities
* Booking channel performance (Net vs Lost Revenue)
* Payment method performance (Revenue Earned vs Lost)

This section highlights revenue leakage, channel profitability, and customer payment behavior.

---

# 🧠 Key Features & Techniques Used

✔ Custom Flip‑Flop KPI Cards
Dynamic KPI cards designed that automatically switch between:

* Positive variance (green, upward trend)
* Negative variance (red, downward trend)

These cards compare current month vs last month for:

* Gross Revenue
* Net Revenue
* Lost Revenue
* % Lost Revenue

✔ Advanced Tooltip Pages
Hovering over any visual element reveals:

* Revenue realized
* Revenue lost
* Channel‑level or nationality‑level breakdown
* Additional contextual metrics

This improves user experience and supports deeper analysis without cluttering the main dashboard.  

✔ Clean Star Schema Data Model

* Fact table for reservations
* Dimension tables for date, customer, channel, nationality, payment method
* Optimized relationships for performance

✔ DAX Highlights
* Time intelligence (MoM, YoY)
* Revenue variance calculations
* Cancellation impact metrics
* Dynamic titles and conditional formatting
